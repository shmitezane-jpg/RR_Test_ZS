Understanding the metrics

  • Mean RR: Average RR interval duration.
  • Mean HR: Average heart rate derived from mean RR.
  • SDNN: Standard deviation of RR intervals, a measure of variability.
  • RMSSD: Root mean square of successive differences, reflects short-term variability.
  • pNN50: Percentage of successive differences greater than 50 ms.
  • Triangular Index: Total number of RR intervals divided by the modal bin height (20 ms bins).
Calculations are intended for educational use and should not replace professional medical advice.
